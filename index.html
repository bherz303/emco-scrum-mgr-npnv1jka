Eval Modes

📋
Board Read — 60 pts total Pulled live from your Monday board. Twelve criteria, 5 pts each. A perfect board scores 60; the lowest possible score is 12, because no criterion scores 0. This is the weekly maintenance read.
⭐
Full Eval Board Read + the Meeting Management items, scored by your RM during or after a sync. Total under review — see the note on that section.

The Bar — 85%, or 51 of 60

85% is the certification line. Letter grades describe your read; the line decides whether you pass it. They are different scales — a B can land on either side of the line, so don't read "B" as "passing."

✓ 51–60 pts · 85%+ PASS
~ 45–50 pts · 75–83% SOFT MISS
✕ 12–44 pts · below 75% MISS

Grade Scale

Descriptive only — the bar is the 85% line above.

A 90–100% Excellent ✓
B 80–89% Strong — 85%+ clears the line, below it doesn't
C 70–79% Needs work
D 60–69% Falling behind
F Below 60% Board needs reset

Fundamentals

10 criteria — 50 pts

Twelve criteria, 5 points each, 60 points total. Every criterion scores 5, 3, or 1 — nothing scores 0. Fundamentals and Conscientious Details are graded on the same scale and count toward the same total; the section names describe what the criteria are about, not how hard they're scored. The certification line is 85% — 51 out of 60.

1
LOW Values on Today Items
5 pts All Today items have a valid Fibonacci LOW — 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, or 21.
3 pts 1 or 2 Today items are missing a LOW or have an invalid value (like 0 or 4).
1 pt 3 or more Today items have no LOW, or LOWs aren't being used at all.

💡 Exception: the Velocity Check pulse is allowed to have a LOW of 0 — it's a tracking pulse, not a worked task, so it never counts against this criterion.

💡 Target DLOW is 15–20 per person per day. A heavy day is worth flagging, but this criterion grades whether the work is sized — not whether the day is overloaded.

2
Pulse Titles
5 pts Titles describe the next action and give enough context to know what the task is.
3 pts A few titles are vague or generic.
1 pt Most titles are things like "Follow up," "Call," or "Meeting" with zero context.

💡 Team shorthand your crew genuinely understands is fine — "ESP," "Dispatch Board," "Courtesy Texts." Only truly meaningless titles get docked.

3
Later Group Dates
5 pts Every Deferred item has a future Monday-specific due date set.
3 pts 1 or 2 deferred items have no date or a non-Monday date.
1 pt 3 or more deferred items with wrong dates — or nothing in Later at all.

💡 Dates must be Mondays — that's when the automation returns items to your Inbox for review.

⚠️ An empty Later group is a miss, not a free pass. Deferring nothing all week means work isn't being categorized — it's either being carried in Today/This Week when it should be deferred, or dropped.

4
Happiness Pulse
5 pts Every manager on the board has one Happiness pulse identified.
3 pts Some managers have one, but not all of them do.
1 pt No manager on the board has one.

💡 One per manager per week. It should be tied to a critical task, a QG pulse, or something you'll genuinely be glad to clear — and it needs to sit in Today or This Week.

💡 On your individual score this is pass/fail: you have one, or you don't. There's no middle grade at the personal level.

5
QG Pulses Visible & QG Value Set
5 pts QG Live pulses are visible in Today or This Week, AND all of them have the QG Value column filled in.
3 pts QG pulses are visible but some are missing QG Value — or values are set but the pulses aren't in an active group.
1 pt QG Value isn't being used on QG pulses at all — or you own no QG pulses.

💡 QG Live pulses parked in Deferred with no date don't count — they need to be in Today or This Week to be visible to the team.

⚠️ Every manager has Q3 rocks cascaded onto their board. If none show up, that's a miss — not "nothing to grade."

6
Inbox Age
5 pts No untriaged pulses older than 5 days sitting in the Inbox. An empty Inbox is a full pass.
3 pts 1 or 2 Inbox items are older than 5 days.
1 pt 3 or more stale items, or the Inbox is being used as a dumping ground.

💡 Triage means: assign it, set a date, move it — don't let things rot in Inbox.

💡 A stale Inbox item is charged to whoever created it. Items with no creator and no assignee go to the BM — triage is the board owner's job.

7
Updates with Context
5 pts All critical pulses — QG, truck/fleet repair and maintenance, sales, customer issues, employee tracking — have an update posted within the last 7 days.
3 pts Most critical pulses have a current update — and/or the gaps are covered by a title plus a Definition of Done that accurately describes the action.
1 pt No current updates and no usable Definition of Done — no evidence of progress.

💡 An update has to be current. A months-old note doesn't satisfy this — the test is current progress, not "has ever been updated."

💡 Sub-items count. If the detail lives in populated sub-items instead of an update post, the context is there and the pulse passes.

💡 Damage pulses and damage claims are excluded — the detail lives in the claim.

🕓 One pass-through, no points off but still flagged for a look: a pulse created today and less than an hour old with no update yet. Too new to expect one.

8
No Duplicates
5 pts One pulse per task, no duplicate entries. A board with nothing duplicable is a full pass.
3 pts 1 or 2 suspected duplicates — or a duplicate correctly spotted but closed instead of archived.
1 pt Clear duplicates sitting on the board.

💡 Finding a duplicate isn't the same as resolving it. Closing it as Done leaves a false completion record while the open copy is still live — archive or delete it instead.

💡 Legitimate repeats aren't duplicates: sales blocks on different days, one onboarding checklist per employee, employee sprint pulses, and Velocity Check copies assigned to different people.

💡 When a duplicate spans two people, it's charged to whoever created the later copy.

9
Automations
5 pts Group distribution matches status — pulses are moving by automation, not by hand.
3 pts Some irregularities affecting active pulses — a few items sitting in the wrong group.
1 pt Items are clearly being dragged manually; statuses and groups don't line up.

💡 Board automations are the BM's to configure, so a broken or missing automation is scored on the BM alone. On your individual score, this criterion only asks one thing: do your pulses' statuses match the group they're sitting in?

💡 A bulk cleanup sweep isn't neglect, and "Needs Review" sitting in Today isn't a mismatch.

10
Definition of Done
5 pts Your Done items carry a meaningful update explaining what was accomplished.
3 pts No completion update, but the title plus the Definition of Done together accurately describe what was done.
1 pt Neither a completion note nor a usable Definition of Done — or, on a live read, nothing was closed at all.

💡 On your mock certification board: the whole Done group is graded, whatever the dates. Add a completion note to every bare item — that's the fix.

💡 On your live board once certified: only the work you closed since the last read is graded. Older Done items aren't re-scored, and a bulk cleanup sweep of long-closed pulses won't count against you. Closing nothing across a full week is a miss.

💡 A real update beats a Definition of Done. Both are accepted — the update is worth full marks, the DoD is partial credit.

💡 Damage pulses and claims are excluded. Sub-item detail counts as documentation.

Conscientious Details

2 criteria — 10 pts

Same 5/3/1 scale, same weight per criterion, same 60-point total. These two are board craft rather than daily fundamentals — they are not optional and they are not scored harder.

11
Project Column
5 pts At least half of active pulses have a Project set, AND all QG and DB Pulse items are correctly tagged.
3 pts At least half filled in but a QG or DB Pulse item is missing its tag — OR fewer than half filled in but all QG and DB Pulse items are correct.
1 pt Fewer than half of active pulses have a Project set AND QG or DB Pulse items are missing their tags.

💡 QG (Quarterly Goal) and DB Pulse (Roundtable) items must always be tagged. Everything else — aim for at least half coverage.

12
No Past-Due Items
5 pts No items in Today or This Week have a past due date.
3 pts 1 or 2 items are past their due date.
1 pt 3 or more past-due items sitting around.

💡 If it's past due, either complete it, defer it with a new Monday date, or delete it. Don't let it linger.

Meeting Management Criteria

Full Eval only — 40 pts
1
Pre-Sync Board Prep Board is prepped 10–15 minutes before the meeting, and the Regional Manager is alerted when it's ready.
5 ptsBoard prepped on time and RM alerted before the meeting starts.
3 ptsBoard prepped but RM wasn't alerted, or it was prepped late.
1 ptBoard wasn't prepped before the meeting.
2
Agenda Flow Meeting follows the standard order — Velocity check, then Hold for Sync, then This Week, then Today, then Inbox cleanup.
5 ptsFollowed the exact agenda order start to finish.
3 ptsMostly followed but skipped a step or went out of order.
1 ptNo clear structure — meeting jumped around with no agenda.
3
Time Management Each person stays within 10 minutes. The meeting doesn't run long because one topic took over.
5 ptsEveryone stayed within 10 minutes. Meeting finished on time.
3 ptsOne person ran over but the meeting recovered.
1 ptMeeting ran long because time wasn't managed.
4
Tabling Discussions Big topics that derail the flow get tabled and followed up on after the sync — not allowed to eat the meeting.
5 ptsOff-topic items were tabled promptly and captured for follow-up.
3 ptsSome derailment happened but was recovered before major damage.
1 ptA tangent took over the meeting and wasn't redirected.
5
Tone The meeting is engaged and constructive. People are present and adding energy — not checked out or combative.
5 ptsEnergy was high, everyone was present and constructive.
3 ptsMostly engaged but some distraction or low energy.
1 ptPeople checked out or the tone was negative or combative.
6
Manager Release The BM releases FMs and Flex managers early when appropriate so they can get to on-sites or sales calls.
5 ptsFMs and Flex released at the right time without being asked.
3 ptsReleased but late, or only after prompting.
1 ptFMs were held through the whole meeting unnecessarily.
7
Automations in Meeting No drag-and-drop during the sync. Pulses move via status changes only — every time.
5 ptsZero drag-and-drop. Every pulse moved via status change.
3 pts1–2 instances of drag-and-drop caught during the meeting.
1 ptDrag-and-drop used throughout — automation logic is broken.
8
Velocity Reviewed The team's velocity is checked and discussed during the sync — DLOW and WLOW are reviewed and the pace for the week is acknowledged.
5 ptsVelocity was reviewed, discussed, and the week's pace acknowledged.
3 ptsVelocity was mentioned but not meaningfully discussed.
1 ptVelocity wasn't covered at all.

5 Things That Move Your Score the Most

1
Set a LOW on every Today item Before your sync — go through Today and make sure every pulse has a valid Fibonacci number. It takes 2 minutes.
2
Tag the Project column on active pulses This is the most commonly missed item. A quick pass through Today assigning project tags gets you easy points.
3
Keep your Velocity pulse active — don't Done it The Velocity pulse should live in Today or This Week permanently. Marking it Done kills your velocity tracking.
4
Always defer to a Monday date When you defer something, set the date to the Monday you plan to revisit it. Any other day breaks the automation.
5
Post a brief update on active pulses Even one sentence — "Waiting on parts," "Called client, no answer" — makes the board tell the story of your work.
💡
Target DLOW of 15–20 Daily Level of Work should be 15 to 20 per person. If Today is overloaded, triage — move lower-priority items to This Week or defer them.

Staying Certified — Weekly Reads, Recovery & Re-test

Certification isn't a one-time badge
1
How you get certified: the mock board Initial certification is earned on your mock training board — 85% or better (51 of 60), with real improvement over your planted baseline. Mock reads are for certifying; they never count toward the maintenance ladder.
2
How you stay certified: a weekly live read Your real board gets read once a week, typically on your sync day, so the score is fresh for the conversation. You'll see it at the sync — it isn't posted to your board before you and your RM have talked. If the schedule shifts and the read lands on a different day, it still counts the same.
3
One miss is not a pattern A single read below the line gets a redirect logged for the team — that's it. No recertification. Everybody has an off week, and one read doesn't tell us anything about a habit.
4
What triggers recertification Either one: three misses inside any four reads (roughly three in a month), or two reads in a row at 44 or below. Same outcome, one path just gets there faster. A pass resets the count.
5
Getting back to certified — Recovery Re-run your own Stage 2 self-check against this reference sheet, fix what's flagged, then score 85%+ on two consecutive weekly reads. Recovery happens on your live board — the point is to fix the real thing, not re-pass a test.
6
Scheduled Re-test — every 6 months Separate from Recovery: six months after your last passing test you re-test on the mock board. That one's a knowledge check. Recovery is the post-miss path. Two different things, deliberately named differently.
7
If you're a BM, you clear two bars Your own individual score and your board's score both have to clear 85%. A spotless personal board on a branch board that's failing isn't the job.
8
Time off doesn't count against you If you're out for the read week, no read gets logged at all — no miss, no pass, nothing on your ladder. You won't get graded on a week you weren't here for.
9
Escalation Hit the recertification trigger twice in the same quarter and the recovery isn't self-serve — your RM runs a live walkthrough with you instead of a remote read.
